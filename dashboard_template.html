People links open LinkedIn or Google searches, so you need to be signed in to LinkedIn. Message LBS alumni first, then the team that owns the role.

  1. Shortlist companiesSports business employers with London or European offices that hire post-MBA talent: consultancies and agencies (including rights and deals advisers), leagues and governing bodies, clubs and teams, sports media and data, and sports investors. The list lives in companies.json.
  2. Read each careers systemJobs are pulled straight from the hiring system behind each careers page (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, SmartRecruiters, Pinpoint, Teamtailor, Workday, BambooHR, UKG, Applied, Oleeo) or read from the company's own vacancies page. A few firms block automated reading or post roles only on LinkedIn; they're marked "check by hand" with links to their careers page and LinkedIn jobs.
  3. FilterLocation: London, rest of UK, Europe, or remote in Europe. Seniority: post-MBA titles (manager, senior manager, lead, consultant, associate), with "Head of" and Director roles flagged as a stretch; interns, graduates, coordinators and VP-level and above are set aside. Function: strategy and consulting, partnerships, media rights, commercial, deals, marketing, product. Engineering, HR, production and matchday roles are marked off-target.
  4. PublishMatching roles appear on the Jobs tab, newest first within each location.
  5. Find peopleEach company and each job has search links for LBS alumni, recruiters and the hiring team.
